Privacy Policy

GeoTag Studio: GPS Map Camera
Developed by The Competitive Edge • Effective Date: September 2026

🛡️ 100% On-Device & Zero-Upload Guarantee:

GeoTag Studio is engineered with a strict offline-first, zero-cloud architecture. All photo capture, image compositing, visual GPS map stamping, coordinate lookups, and binary EXIF metadata injection occur entirely inside your device memory. No photos, location data, or device identifiers are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers or cloud databases.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GeoTag Studio: GPS Map Camera ("we", "our", or "the application") handles user privacy when you use our Android mobile application and web utility. We provide free, privacy-first, professional tools for educational institutions (NAAC/UGC documentation, campus activities), civil engineering field inspections, site verifications, government audits, and general photography.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Because GeoTag Studio operates as a self-contained, on-device utility:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, or user identities.
  • No Image or Photo Uploads: Photos you load, capture, or export remain strictly in your local device memory and are cleared when you close or refresh the app.
  • No Location Tracking or History: Your GPS coordinates are queried solely on-demand to stamp the photo you are currently editing. We do not track, log, profile, or store your historical movements.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising SDKs: We do not include Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, Facebook SDK, or any tracking/telemetry libraries.
  • No Financial Data: The application is 100% free with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payment gateways.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

GeoTag Studio requests only the minimal permissions necessary to perform its advertised geotagging features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Optional runtime permission. Used exclusively when you choose to take a photo directly inside the app using your device camera.
  • Location (android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Optional runtime permission. Used solely when you click the "My Current GPS" button to determine your current latitude, longitude, and approximate address for the photo stamp. Coordinates are never transmitted to any third party.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely to download public map tiles (OpenStreetMap / Esri) for the mini-map preview and reverse geocoding lookups. When offline, the app automatically generates an offline vector grid and compass map without requiring internet access.
  • Photo Library Access: Handled securely via Android's built-in System Photo Picker. The app does not request broad media read permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES), ensuring maximum user privacy.

4. Data Storage & Local Persistence

Custom favorite locations you explicitly save (e.g. your campus or office coordinates) and your stamp appearance preferences are stored locally on your device using HTML5 localStorage. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the application cache in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

GeoTag Studio is intended for general audiences, college students, educators, and field professionals. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • Data Collection: No user data is collected.
  • Data Sharing: No user data is shared with third parties.
  • Security Practices: All data processing is strictly local (100% on-device).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
