📲 Install GeoTag Studio

In Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge:

  1. Click the Three Vertical Dots (⋮) at the top-right corner of your browser (beside your profile icon).
  2. Select "Save and share" → click "Install GeoTag Studio..." (or directly "Install GeoTag Studio..." in the menu list).
  3. Click Install when prompted. GeoTag Studio will open in its own clean window and add a desktop icon!
💡 1-Click Offline Launcher: On Windows, you can also simply double-click GeoTag-Desktop.bat in your project folder!

On iPhone or iPad using Safari:

  1. Open Safari and navigate to this site.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up: [ ↑ ]).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ([ + ]).
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner.
✅ The GeoTag Studio app icon will appear directly on your iPhone home screen and open full-screen like a native app!
